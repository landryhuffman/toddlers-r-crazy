    About this project

    How posts were sorted

    A researcher read and hand-coded a random sample of posts to build a codebook of child behaviors, context, parent experiences and what parents were asking for. An AI model (Claude) then applied that codebook to every post, and its codes were checked against the researcher's on the hand-coded sample. Posts about children under 1 or over 5, product recommendations, and general questions not about a specific child are left out.

    What this can and can't tell you

    • It shows what parents post about. People post when something is hard or surprising, so these counts reflect worry, not how common a behavior is in all toddlers.
    • Reddit users aren't representative of all parents.
    • Automated coding makes mistakes, so a few posts may land under the wrong topic.
    • Nothing here is medical advice.

    Privacy

    This site shows only post titles and links back to Reddit. It doesn't copy post text or usernames. If someone deletes their post, the link stops working.
